Yes! *Scam With Your Friends* is an upcoming indie co-op simulation game developed by JakeHub and Metater (published by Jatater Worldwide). It blew up on social media, quickly passing 100,000 wishlists on Steam.

The game puts you and your crew in charge of a chaotic scam call center:

* **Real-time AI voice interactions:** You talk directly to fictional, procedurally generated AI callers over your mic to pull off absurd scam scripts and drain their savings.
* **Scambaiters and office threats:** Victims fight back, scambaiters try to inject malware or counter-hack your setup, and you have to dodge police raids while hitting daily targets.
* **Angry boss reviews:** At the end of every shift, you face harsh performance reviews from a rage-filled manager based on whether your desk hit its quota.
* **Modding and customization:** The developers are adding custom caller personalities using JSON configs and voice cloning files.

The game targets an Early Access launch on Steam for October 2026.
